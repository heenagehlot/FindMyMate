04 GOOD TO KNOW

A few common questions.

Is FindMyMate a live roommate service?

No. This is a frontend prototype with fictional profiles. It has no real accounts, listings, matches, or messaging.

Are the compatibility highlights validated?

No. They are illustrative examples only and are not a guarantee or scientific compatibility score.

Can I enter my real details?

Please do not. Form submissions are local demonstrations; no information is sent to a service.

Does the prototype store passwords?

No. Passwords are cleared from forms and are never saved in browser storage.
